COACHING + CLINICAL SUPERVISION

Clinical Supervision & Coaching

Are you committed to understanding & exposing racism in both your personal life and work life?

It’s important for white people to have a healing space to explore internalized racial superiority and white privilege. Through examining both the intent and consequences of their actions you will begin the lifelong journey of accountability and antiracist practices. Together, we will:

  • Develop a deeper awareness of how power and privilege affect the way you navigate and experience the world

  • Become better at cultivating more authentic relationships across race and culture
