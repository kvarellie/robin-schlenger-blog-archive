July Reflections: Untangling Freedom and Responsibility on the 4th

As a white cis woman committed to anti-racism, the 4th of July brings up more than just fireworks and flags—it brings questions. This holiday, often framed as a celebration of freedom, asks something different of those of us reckoning with the legacy of whiteness and the history of this nation. Whose freedom was declared in 1776? Whose voices were silenced then—and continue to be now?

What does it mean to be free in a country built on stolen land and forced labor? What does it mean to benefit from systems designed to exclude, while working to dismantle them? What does it mean to be a patriot?

I invite us into an honest exploration of how white people have historically upheld—and can actively challenge—the myths of American freedom. July 4th is a chance not just to remember history, but to interrogate our place within it. We have a lot of work to do!

See you on the journey,

Robin
