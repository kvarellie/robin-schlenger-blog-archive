On Cooking Together.

Every Tuesday, we wrap up work at the studio and go to the nearby food co-op to pick out seasonal produce and locally-sourced meats. Our usual go-to for a quick, home-cooked meal is a kitchen sink stew—perfect for the coming fall. Here's are latest recipe. Enjoy!

  • 2 Condimentum est
  • 1/4 Aenean viverra, interdum turpis
  • Fusce scelerisque orci
  • Orci a lobortis sollicitudin
  • Praesent condimentum

Recipe

  1. In hac habitasse platea dictumst.
  2. Proin fringilla, est non fermentum cursus.
  3. Fusce scelerisque orci
  4. Orci a lobortis sollicitudin
  5. That’s it!
