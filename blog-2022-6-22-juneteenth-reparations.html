Juneteenth Reparations

I love summer and the warm weather, and I got to celebrate another rotation around the sun on June 19th. I’m honored to share my birthday with the celebration of Juneteenth and Father's Day this year. I have been in many conversations about reparations recently and thought that this month would be a good time to share some resources to help with moving from thinking to doing. The last article in the list of this month's resources share some very concrete action steps that white people can do to help celebrate Juneteenth. 

See you on the journey,

Robin
